## 3. Fixes, most likely first

1. **Make large views sectioned or summarised.** `/api/initiative` returned 158 KB for a Community. Give it a summary by default (counts per section, top items) and `?section=plan|tools|related|outputs` with paging. The skill should say to save the response and read sections, not print it. This would have exposed SKM and the plant pilot. It is a general fix: 020 and 001 both lost facts to big responses, and other runs likely did too.
2. **Add a derived `SharedWork` table** (the same as fix 3 in 001.md): pairs of Communities with what they share (project, bio.tools tag, Recommends resource, output, person in roles on both sides, Community–Community statement) and the source. "Who should Systems Biology partner with" becomes one query ranked by evidence type. This is the general version of components 1–5.
3. **Link plan items to what they name.** Plan items extracted from roadmaps and reports should get edges to the tools, resources and Communities they name (for example, "Stress Knowledge Map" → `tool:skm` → Plant Sciences; "PBPK models" → Toxicology).
4. **Type programme areas distinctly** (`programme_area`), so CMR, HDTR and BFSP do not pass as joint projects. Split RelatedTo into partner, parent and mention, or at least set a `relation` field for "listed under programme area".
