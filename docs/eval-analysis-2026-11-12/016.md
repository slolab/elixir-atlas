## 2. Root cause of the missed component

### C2 "ten recommendations": HARD_TO_REACH (the items are present but ungrouped), with a publication-level DATA_MISSING contribution

What is in the graph (checked):

- The ten recommendations exist as ten separate `Resource` rows of kind `practice`, each a `Recommends` row with basis `recommends` from `https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.13127`. The evidence fields are numbered: "page 18: 1 Use modular and transparent design", "2 Use standardized, open formats", … "page 19: 10 Share simulation protocols and parameters". They sit among 42 `Recommends` rows from that preprint. No field groups them, says "ten" or names the list.
  ```sql
  SELECT res.name, res.kind, r.basis, r.evidence FROM Recommends r JOIN Resource res ON res.id=r.target
  WHERE r.source_url='https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.13127' ORDER BY r.evidence;  -- 42 rows, 10 numbered practices
  ```
- The preprint has no `Output` record:
  ```sql
  SELECT * FROM Output WHERE url LIKE '%2609.13127%'  -- 0 rows
  ```
  Outputs come only from Zenodo and F1000. The preprint appears as:
  - two `PlanItem`s with status done: `plan:systems-biology/output-making-models-that-matter` and the SYBEL plan item. Their descriptions read "A preprint on building trustworthy and useful systems biology models…" and "A paper summarising and integrating FAIR and CURE guidelines…", and neither mentions ten recommendations.
  - one `Resource` `resource:making-models-that-matter`, kind document, basis `develops`.
- The phrase exists upstream. The arXiv abstract (`data/input/systems-biology/abs-2609.13127-9600566b7535.html`, in the scraping index) ends: "we propose ten recommendations for building and sharing systems biology models that are both FAIR- and CURE-compliant". The abstract page is not ingested anywhere. A search of every text column in graph.sqlite for "ten recommendations" matches only an unrelated bioimaging F1000 title.

## 3. Fixes

1. **Make publications that groups produce or link first-class `Output`s, whatever the host.** This covers arXiv, bioRxiv and journals linked from Community pages or named in reports. Store the title, abstract, authors and `OutputOf` basis `linked`. The arXiv abstract would carry "ten recommendations" directly, and the author list would add preprint authors as Community people, which also helps 019.
2. **Group list-structured recommendations.** When a document's recommendations form a numbered list, record `list_name` (e.g. "Ten recommendations for FAIR- and CURE-compliant models") and `position` on each `Recommends` row. Alternatively, add a summary row on the `SourceDocument` or `Output` ("proposes 10 recommendations: …").
3. **View:** a `/api/output` or `/api/document` detail that returns a publication with its `Recommends` children, ordered by position. One request then answers "what does this paper recommend".
