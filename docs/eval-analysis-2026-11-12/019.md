## 3. Fixes

1. **Derived `MemberOf`/`NamedIn` edge (person → initiative).** Give it `basis` role | plan_item | author | related_project_role, plus `source_url`. Fold in the group's predecessor (`became`) and closely related commissioned services such as SYBEL. Then "people of the Systems Biology Community and their tools" is a single join to `CreditedForTool`. Add a `/api/initiative` `people` section that carries the "member lists are not public; lower bound from these sources" note, so the disclaimer arrives with the data.
2. **Resolve name-split duplicates when one side has only a bio.tools credit.** If all of a name's other records are joinable (same name, compatible email domain or ORCID on one side only), merge them, or at least add a `same_as_candidate` column that queries can follow. Anton is the visible case here.
3. **Keep institutional credits on tools.** Add `Tool.credited_organisations`, or a `CreditedForTool` edge to `Organisation`.
4. **Ingest group-linked preprints as `Output`s with `Authored`** (see 016). This adds preprint authors to the named people, and through the admission rule their tools.
5. **Add `basis` to the initiative view's `tools` list.** It currently mixes the 3 tags and 56 domain tools without distinction (`views.py` `initiative()` `tools` query).
